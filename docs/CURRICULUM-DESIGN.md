## 0. Design laws (non-negotiable)

- **Sensing honesty.** Scored channels: PACE, LINE, CARRIAGE (shipped) + HOLD, TURN, BEAT (engine-measured; surfacing per the display-before-gate law below). Nothing anywhere claims to measure feet, hips, arms, face, eyes, or expression [P5]. **No scored criterion or statistic string may contain gaze/eye/face/body language — head kinematics only; where a head measure proxies a craft skill, the proxy is stated in copy** ("the head holds the focal point — the eyes are yours" [DESIGN]). Unmeasured skills are *coached by cue only* and every such cue is labeled so, in drills and levels alike.
- **No invented claims.** Every criterion, drill, and copy string traces to a finding or carries [DESIGN]. The F-record's do-not-cite list is binding. **Lint law:** a pre-ship pass checks every attribution string verbatim against its finding's caveats, blocks every do-not-print phrasing at the copy worksheet, and verifies that every [F#]/[P#] tag resolves.
- **Display-before-gate.** No channel may gate, be selected as the "weakest channel," or appear in a level criterion until its score is displayed to the user at that level [DESIGN; Aurora transparency, F12]. There is no such thing as an invisible criterion.
- **Outcome marking.** We score the observable head-kinematic outcome, never the unobserved means of production — the precedent that makes marking consistent [F12iii]; the transfer of that precedent to walking is ours [DESIGN].
- **No BPM claims.** Tempo is always musical [P2] and relative to the user's own baseline [F11b shape, DESIGN]; no industry BPM is ever printed [P7].
- **Source prominence.** Commercial-school, marketplace, and SEO-grade sources are never named in learner-facing copy. Their names and caveats live on the lineage page only, under a no-affiliation / no-endorsement notice (covering John Casablancas, Wilhelmina, ABRSM, Aurora, the FTC). Learner-facing named lineage is reserved for the P-record coaches and the peer-reviewed literature [DESIGN].
- **Measurement candor.** Until per-channel repeatability / noise-floor figures are published on the lineage page, shipped copy says "measured" or "instrumented," never "objective" [DESIGN; R1 note]. Publishing those figures is a shipping gate (§5).
- **Safety.** Heels content unlocks only after the flat-shoe Level 1 pass; every heels session requires the boundary / clear-runway check; heel height ladders gradually; a safety disclosure ships beside the honesty disclosures [DESIGN]. No score is worth a fall.

## 1. THE CURRICULUM SPINE

**Pattern:** a graded syllabus with ONE small, stable criterion vocabulary at every level — difficulty scales through content and band tightness, never through new criteria (modeled on ABRSM's five-areas-across-all-grades structure [F12i]; the transfer is ours [DESIGN]). Our fixed vocabulary is the six honest channels. Advancement is evidence-of-mastery, not seat time [F12 Aurora]; expectations are explicit, transparent, measurable [F12 Aurora].

**Channel surfacing (resolves the former open call).** Recommended to the founder: each hidden channel becomes a visible scored channel at the level where it first appears in a criterion — BEAT at Level 2, HOLD and TURN at Level 3 — so the display grows with the syllabus, the same difficulty-through-content pattern [F12i, DESIGN]. **Backstop, whatever the founder decides:** under the display-before-gate law, an unsurfaced channel cannot gate, cannot be picked as "weakest," and its criteria are marked "activates when surfaced"; until then Levels 2–4 gate on PACE/LINE/CARRIAGE only and D4–D6 run as labeled cue-only practice.

**Content axes that scale across levels [DESIGN]:** route (straight → shaped), tempo (one track → contrasting tracks), ending (stop → pose → pivot → variants), **footwear** (flats → low block heel → show heel; a flats/menswear parallel track keeps every level walkable without heels), and conditions (coached → cold). Every footwear change triggers a PACE/LINE re-baseline (§3) — heels legitimately shift cadence and drift, and the bands must acknowledge it [DESIGN].

**Entry is by placement, not by default** — see §4 (the placement walk). Our structural model is open-entry [F12 caveat]; nobody grinds a beginner level they can already pass.

Five levels [DESIGN — names are founder-approvable drafts]:

### Level 1 — THE LINE
The beginner checklist, honestly scored [P1]. Content: straight runway, flat shoes, one slow track — and a taught ending from day one: decelerate over the final zone and stop facing front (no pose, no pivot yet) [DESIGN; the decelerate phase is P4's]. A walk always has an ending; Level 3 upgrades it rather than installing one against a habit.
- **LINE:** "Tracks the center of the catwalk — lateral drift stays inside the Level-1 band for ≥N% of walk time." [F2 (the center-of-catwalk rehearsal cue); band value + N% DESIGN, pending on-device calibration.] *"The invisible line" (one-foot-in-front foot placement [P1]) is cue-only coach copy, labeled coached-not-measured [P5] — foot placement is not what this sensor sees.*
- **CARRIAGE:** "Holds a fixed focal point — head pitch and yaw steady inside the band." [P1. Proxy stated in copy: the head holds the focal point; the eyes are yours (DESIGN).] *"Keep your gaze forward, never down" is cue-only coach copy, labeled coached-not-measured [F11d corroboration; P5].*
- **PACE:** "Keeps one felt tempo through the walk zone — speed inside a wide band around the walker's own baseline; the band windows out the final deceleration zone." [Musical delivery P2; own-baseline offset shape adapted from running gait retraining, F11b, DESIGN; the deceleration window is DESIGN.]
- **Cue-only track [P1, P5]:** posture, stride length, arm carriage, expression — coached in copy, labeled "coached, never scored," at this and every level.

### Level 2 — THE WALK
Same criteria, tighter bands, plus tempo as content [F12i pattern, DESIGN]. Content: two contrasting tracks — a slow, sustained track and a brisk, driving track [DESIGN wording; multi-tempo training exists in published commercial-school materials, as published by that school — F1, named on the lineage page only]; targets stay relative-to-baseline [DESIGN]. Footwear: a low block heel enters as content [DESIGN; heels cues per the corroborated convention F11c, cue-only P5; safety law applies; re-baseline on the footwear change, §3].
- **PACE:** "Finds the new tempo within M meters of the music change and holds the band." [DESIGN]
- **BEAT (surfaces at this level per the display-before-gate law; backstop: activates when surfaced):** "Cadence locks to the felt beat — 'don't lose the rhythm'" (vocabulary: Russo's rehearsal cue, PFW SS11 diary across three shows [F2]).
- **LINE/CARRIAGE:** Level-1 wording, narrower bands [DESIGN].
- Once each track is held solo, the two tempos interleave within a block — variety of already-learned content, not new mechanics [DESIGN; F5 load-matching frame].

### Level 3 — THE TURN
End-of-runway craft [P4]. Content: the end-of-runway mark [DESIGN vocabulary], the pose, and the standard half turn — plus the show heel entering as content [DESIGN; safety law; re-baseline].
- **HOLD (surfaces at this level; backstop as above):** "Decelerates, then keeps the head still for 2–3 seconds at the runway's end — stillness inside the band. (The pose itself is your craft; we measure the stillness.)" [P4; proxy stated, DESIGN.]
- **TURN (surfaces at this level; backstop as above):** "The turn is unhurried and controlled — clean segmentation, duration inside a band that favors slower, smoother pivots; the head leaves the front focal point last, and the head re-faces the return focal point before the first return strides settle." [P4; wording and re-acquisition mechanic DESIGN. "Re-acquired" is defined as head orientation re-facing the target — never eyes.]
- **Cue-only track:** the pose's body line, hands, weight, expression — coached, never scored [P5].

### Level 4 — THE SHOW
No new mechanics — consistency under show conditions [F12i pattern, DESIGN]. Content: full routes (straight/T/X/U) with mid-route corners now trained first (D7); full and double-half turn variants enter as content, descriptively named [DESIGN — no cross-school turn taxonomy exists (open question 6); we adopt no school's names]; entrance-on-cue and interval work behind a paced ghost (D8); show heel required for cold blocks [DESIGN]. In-walk coaching feedback OFF; direction is limited to one terse pre-walk line in the rehearsal register [F2].
- **All displayed channels:** "Holds Level-3 bands on the cold block" [§3 canonical definition; F4 + the guidance-hypothesis lineage F7; the day-apart gap and 3-walk block are our conventions, DESIGN — user copy says so].
- **Repeatability:** "Walk-to-walk spread inside the consistency band." [DESIGN — consistency as a mastery criterion is our choice, consonant with the professional premium on reproducibility; F7b is motivation only, not warrant.]
- **Transfer probe:** one never-practiced route/tempo combination, scored cold, feedback off, recorded beside the retention median [F4 names retention OR transfer; probe design DESIGN; F8's transfer-to-untrained-tasks is the precedent shape].

### Level 5 — THE SIGNATURE
Individuation, not new mechanics [P6]. Criteria INVERT deliberately: the walker declares a personal profile (e.g., a slower own-tempo, a signature carriage envelope) and is scored on holding *their own* declared bands [DESIGN]. **Anti-gaming floor:** declared bands must sit at or tighter than the Level-4 pass bands on every channel, and the record displays the declared band beside the score — a gamed profile is self-evident [DESIGN]. Two reference-flavor archetypes may be described as "two documented development-board flavors — a relaxed walk and a confident walk" [DESIGN, adapted from a 2016 agency profile; the agency's name and citation live on the lineage page only, under the no-affiliation notice; F14d]. We never claim these are current agency doctrine [F14d caveat]. Level 5 ends with the graduation-truth disclosure (§5, Disclosure 6).

**Spine-wide rules:** every criterion names its channel; every band value is a labeled [DESIGN] constant pending on-device calibration; no criterion at any level references feet, hips, arms, eyes, or expression [P5]; every level carries its labeled cue-only track; no channel gates before it is displayed (design law); levels have **no stated duration** — the 8-session/2-week shape (§3) governs feedback-fade blocks, and a level may take as many blocks as it takes [F8 caveat, DESIGN].

## 2. THE DRILLS

**Drill kernel laws:**
- Drills are whole-walk with a single scored-channel FOCUS, or sequential-phase segments — never fractionated simultaneous components practiced in isolation [F6; the walking transfer is DESIGN]. Phase segmentation backward-chains: ending alone → final-meters+ending → full route ("the variant supported in the reviewed part-task studies" [F6], contested later [F6 caveat]). Part-task work reintegrates to whole walks within the same session [F6].
- Blocked repetition is the novice default — the high-CI evidence is contested and two meta-analyses disagree [F5]. Within Levels 2–3, already-held content is interleaved serially inside blocks (alternating learned tempos, alternating learned endings) [DESIGN; challenge-point implies difficulty tracks skill, F5 caveat]; full variability enters as content at L4+.
- One cued channel at L1–2, two at L3–4, three at L5 — our design extrapolation from the load-matching framework [F5 W&S, DESIGN].
- **Feedback, two distinct classes [F7 caveat]:** (1) *Terminal, post-walk feedback* fades on reduced average frequency [F7a] with bandwidth silence inside tolerance ("silence means you were in the band" [F7b]). (2) *Concurrent guidance* — the beacon, the drift rail, in-walk beat haptics — withdraws on the concurrent-guidance lineage (Schmidt & Wulf 1997, per the F7 caveat; the runway transfer is DESIGN). Both are strong DEFAULTS, user-controllable. **Restoration triggers only on retention evidence — cold-block decline across two or more sessions — never on a within-session dip** [DESIGN; the withdrawal dip IS the learning/performance distinction, F4]; coach copy normalizes it ("scores usually drop when the rail goes quiet — that's expected, keep walking" [F.40 DRAFT]).
- All cue phrasing is external-focus (the line, the beacon, the beat — never "your neck/hips/knees"): our design choice consistent with the literature; no learning-gain promise [F10].
- Rest between reps: the short (<1 min) heuristic applies to short-segment drills only (D1 reps, D4/D5 chained segments) — it comes from the simple-psychomotor cluster [F9]; rest is unprescribed for full-walk drills, which map to the complex clusters [F9 caveat].
- Every drill states its cue-only elements explicitly (design law).

**Starter set (per-drill: lineage → prescription → statistic → honesty label):**

**D1 · TAPE LINE (LINE).** Lineage: structure adapted from a commercial modeling school's published home routine — tape line, 10-step reps, review step, 5–10 minute sessions [F1 — the school is named on the lineage page only] + the beginner line checklist item [P1]. Prescription: 3–5 blocked reps of a 10-step line walk; the drift rail withdraws on the concurrent-guidance lineage [F7 caveat, DESIGN]. Statistic: LINE time-in-band. Label: **HEAD-MEASURED · SHIPPED.** (The source's heel-to-toe foot cue is neither borrowed nor scored [F1 caveat, P5].)

**D2 · FOCAL POINT (CARRIAGE).** Lineage: fixed focal point / steady head [P1]; spotting pedagogy favors a fixed external target over a mirror [F11a] — our world-anchored AR beacon is our analog of that target [DESIGN]. Prescription: full walks with the beacon; the beacon withdraws on the concurrent-guidance lineage [F7 caveat, DESIGN] and **fades TO something, not to nothing** — it hands off to a real fixed feature ("pick a spot on your wall at eye level — that is your camera now" [F.40 DRAFT]), the stated transfer goal [F11a, DESIGN]. Statistic: CARRIAGE time-in-band (head pitch/yaw steadiness — the head holds the focal point; the eyes are yours [DESIGN]). Label: **HEAD-MEASURED · SHIPPED.**

**D3 · OWN TEMPO (PACE).** Lineage: pace is taught musically [P2]; the percent-offset-from-own-baseline SHAPE is adapted from running gait retraining [F11b — shape only; its injury-load purpose has no runway analogue, so the purpose here is ours: DESIGN]. **Goal: tempo control, not tempo change** — a controllable range in both directions from baseline, motivated by documented multi-tempo training [F1] and the documented pace tension [P3]. Prescription: capture baseline per the §3 protocol; train deliberate offsets in the direction of the walker's diagnosed tendency — habitual rushers train downward (hold the glide), draggers upward (find the brisk track) [DESIGN; P3 is why direction is chosen per user]; offsets capped at single-digit %, felt through music/haptics, never printed as BPM [P7]; 2–3 sessions/week echoing the source protocol's shape [F11b]. Statistic: PACE time-in-band. Label: **HEAD-MEASURED · SHIPPED.** The ~125 spm-vs-anti-rush tension [P3] is coach-copy context, never a target.

**D4 · THE MARK (HOLD).** (Renamed from "the pit pause" — "the pit" is the photographers' pit; the commercial school's term lives in the research record only [F1 ruling].) Lineage: decelerate → 2–3 s pose [P4]. Prescription: backward-chained segment — the held ending alone, then final-meters+ending, then full walk [F6]; blocked 4–6 reps [DESIGN]. Statistic: **head stillness duration during the pose** (the pose itself is craft, cue-only [P5]). Label: **HEAD-MEASURED · SURFACES AT L3** (backstop: cue-only practice until surfaced).

**D5 · LEAVE LAST (TURN).** (Rebuilt: the former spotting-whip version is retired — spotting is fast-repeated-rotation technique and contradicts the deliberate single pivot [F11a ruling, P4].) Lineage: the end-of-runway protocol — decelerate → pose → pivot with the head leaving last [P4]; re-acquisition inspiration borrowed from spotting pedagogy, labeled borrowed [F11a, DESIGN]. Prescription: backward-chained — the pivot alone, then pose+pivot, then walk+pose+pivot+return [F6]; the head lingers on the front focal point and leaves last, then re-faces the return focal point before the first return strides settle; 4–10 reps per direction (rep counts are our design inside the corroborated envelope [F11a caveat, DESIGN]); **no rotation-speed ladder — the band favors slower, smoother turns; if anything ladders, it is pose-hold duration or music tempo [DESIGN]**. Statistic: TURN segmentation, duration (slow-favoring band), and head re-acquisition time. Cue-only elements, labeled: body-rotation mechanics, weight transfer — the scored statistic is head kinematics exclusively [P5]. Label: **HEAD-MEASURED · SURFACES AT L3** (backstop as D4). *An optional off-runway spotting exercise may ship separately as gaze-discipline conditioning, explicitly labeled borrowed ballet conditioning, not turn technique [F11a, DESIGN].*

**D6 · BEAT LOCK (BEAT).** Lineage: musical coaching as documented in a 2016 agency profile [F14d — agency named on the lineage page only]; "don't lose the rhythm" [F2]; pace-as-music law [P2]. Prescription: full walks with beat haptics; in-walk haptics withdraw on the concurrent-guidance lineage [F7 caveat, DESIGN]; post-walk beat stats fade on reduced average frequency [F7a]. Statistic: BEAT phase-lock time-in-band. Label: **HEAD-MEASURED · SURFACES AT L2** (backstop as D4).

**D7 · THE CORNER (TURN + LINE) — new.** Lineage: shaped routes contain mid-route corners — a distinct skill (turn while sustaining tempo and line; no pose, no pause) [DESIGN]. Prescription: backward-chained [F6] — corner alone → leg+corner → full route; blocked reps. Statistic: TURN segmentation through the corner + LINE re-acquisition on the new leg. Label: **HEAD-MEASURED · SURFACES AT L4** (backstop as D4). L4 never gates on an untrained skill: this drill precedes any shaped-route benchmark.

**D8 · THE INTERVAL (PACE) — new.** Lineage: show discipline is largely spacing — the rehearsal record is group direction ("use the center of the catwalk only, shoulder to shoulder" [F2]); the drill mechanic is our design use of the existing pace-ghost feed-forward tech [DESIGN — no new sourcing claims]. Prescription: entrance on cue, then hold the interval behind a paced ghost for the full walk. Statistic: PACE relative to a moving reference. Label: **HEAD-MEASURED · SHIPPED (L4 content).**

**Footwear & safety:** the footwear ladder is spine content (§1), not a footnote. In heels modes, coach copy flips to the corroborated heels convention — smaller steps, no lean, eyes up [F11c, corroboration tier] — overriding the long-strides beginner cue [P1]; the conflict is real and documented [F11c]. Cue-only, never scored [P5]. Heels sessions enforce the safety law (§0): flat-shoe L1 pass, boundary/clear-runway check, gradual heel-height ladder [DESIGN].

**Fault library (coach copy only):** looking down; rushed turn; pace faults cut both ways — attributed as "one agency's coaching list" [F11d, corroboration tier]. **"Bouncing walk" is the exception: vertical head oscillation IS head-measurable — it is labeled "not currently scored" (never "never scored"), with a stated rationale ("vertical smoothness is engine-feasible but deferred [DESIGN]") and a logged founder call for a CARRIAGE extension or seventh channel (open question 8).**

## 3. THE SESSION PLANNER

**Canonical cold-block definition (used everywhere, stated only here):** *Cold block = one unscored re-entry walk, then the next three scored walks, in a session at least a day after the last, with all feedback off. The median of the three is the retention score.* The discard, the N=3, and the day-apart gap are our conventions [DESIGN], per the F4 warm-up-decrement caveat and the feedback-off guidance-hypothesis lineage [F4, F7]. User copy phrases the gap as our rule ("a later session, at least a day apart — our convention"), never as a research threshold [F4 caveat].

**Baseline governance [DESIGN — everything downstream hangs on it]:**
- Capture = median of N cold walks under stated conditions (route, track, footwear) [N pending noise-floor data].
- Separate baselines per footwear mode — heels shift cadence and drift honestly.
- Re-baseline at every level entry, after every footwear change, and after each 1-month re-walk.
- The protocol publishes on the lineage page like everything else.

**Session shape** [each step tagged]:
1. **Warm-up walk** — the cold block's unscored re-entry walk (canonical definition above), or a plain unscored opener on non-benchmark days [F4 caveat; DESIGN].
2. **Weakest-displayed-channel drill block** — 8–12 min, blocked reps [F5], short rests for short-segment drills only [F9 caveat], feedback per the kernel's two-class rules [F7].
3. **Full walk(s)** — whole-task reintegration, required every session [F6].
4. **Review** — replay of your measured channels [DESIGN]. *If the optional phone-camera body capture is active, this becomes body-video review — and only then may the published review-step pedagogy be cited [F1, F14d]; otherwise those citations are not earned and are not used.* The disclosures state plainly that mirror/video self-review and a human coach's eye remain irreplaceable (§5, Disclosure 6).

**Dosage:** ~15–20 min/session [DESIGN; envelope between the published 5–10 min drill sessions [F1] and the modeled protocol's session lengths [F8]]; 3–4 sessions/week. The **feedback-fade block** — ~8 sessions over ~2 weeks with feedback fading in the back half — is modeled on a published gait-retraining protocol (n=10, clinical, running, no control — precedent, not proof) [F8]. **A fade block is not a level: levels have no stated duration and may take many blocks** [F8 caveat, DESIGN].

**Benchmark cadence:**
- **Cold block** at the first session after each day-plus gap (canonical definition above) — the only source of Mastery Record and gate evidence [F4, F7].
- **Practice check** (renamed from "level benchmark") — an optional weekly in-practice check, run warm, feedback on; **barred from the Mastery Record and from all gate evidence** — it is an acquisition snapshot, not retention [F4, DESIGN].
- **Transfer probe** at Level 4 — never-practiced route/tempo, cold, feedback off, recorded beside the retention median [F4; DESIGN].
- **1-month re-walk** after a level pass — honest hook: the protocol we modeled dosage on found partial drift back toward baseline by 1 month [F8]. **Consequence policy [DESIGN]:** the level pass stands; the record is annotated ("held at pass" / "drifted at 1 mo"); a short refresher block of the weakest displayed channel's drill is recommended — mirroring the source's finding that partially-faded feedback maintained most mechanics [F8].

## 4. THE LADDER & GATES

**Entry — the placement walk [DESIGN; open-entry precedent F12 caveat]:** onboarding includes one full-route session scored cold across all displayed channels, mapped against the level bands, producing a soft-recommended entry level. It doubles as the initial baseline (§3). Nobody is forced through THE LINE who can already pass it.

**Gate mechanics** (pattern from ABRSM, transfer is ours [F12, DESIGN]):
- Criterion-referenced, no quotas, thresholds published up front, "marked up from the pass line" framing [F12ii].
- **Floors first:** every channel must clear its floor; above the floors, strength in one channel may balance weakness in another [F12ii; floors DESIGN]. **The level's namesake channel is non-compensable — its band is both the floor and the pass bar at that level** [DESIGN — a deliberate tightening of the ABRSM transfer, not ABRSM's rule]. You cannot pass THE LINE while drifting off the line.
- Gate evidence = the cold block (§3 canonical), never a live session best and never a practice check [F4].
- **Two-number display, Mastery Record first:** the Mastery Record (cold-block median) is the headline number; "today's best" is the secondary context line [F4; DESIGN — the number the curriculum argues matters gets the headline weight].
- **Soft-recommend default:** the app recommends promotion and records the evidence; it never hard-blocks [DESIGN]. One prerequisite-style recommendation exists: entering THE SIGNATURE recommends a THE SHOW pass, with a stated rationale sentence — the pattern (gate + published pedagogical rationale) is ABRSM's [F12iv]; ours is a recommendation, not a bar [DESIGN].

**Gate wording rules [DESIGN, bound by F-record]:**
- Never "fail" — "Not yet. Your record stands at …"
- Every gate names its channel, band, and evidence ("held cold, a day later, three walks" — the canonical block in plain words).
- "Better indicator" language only — never "the only valid measure of learning" [F4].
- No seat-time gates ever [F12 Aurora].
- Every gate string routes through F.40; no lab dialect ships.

**What may NEVER gate:** any channel not displayed at that level (design law); composites (display-only); any unmeasured skill — feet, hips, arms, eyes, expression [P5]; a single cold walk [F4]; a practice check (§3); anything worded as a career outcome [F14]; any music-BPM target [P7].

## 5. RESPECTABILITY DESIGN

**What the gatekeeper research says to avoid:**
- Pay-for-credential patterns: paid runway instruction sits in the pay-in-advance modeling-school category the FTC's scam-warning guidance covers — we never sell advancement, certificates, or "career readiness" [F14a].
- Placement promises: guaranteed work or salaries are the FTC's named scam markers [F14b]. Zero career-outcome claims anywhere.
- Signing claims: a scouting firm states classes don't determine fashion signing [F14c] — cited narrowly, never universalized; we never imply training → getting signed.
- Credential inflation — including **by association**: no commercial school's or agency's name in learner-facing copy (design law); the surveyed market's only "credentials" are completion certificates with no skill assessment [F13c]. We ship **progress records, not credentials** [DESIGN].
- Unverifiable authority: no "industry standard" phrasing without a finding; no universal claims from survey-scoped negatives [F3, F13]; no "objective" before the noise-floor figures publish (design law).

**How respect is earned instead [DESIGN, built on F12/F13/F14]:**
- **A tiered public lineage page:** sources grouped as (1) named coaches and casting/agency practice [P#, F14d], (2) peer-reviewed motor-learning literature, (3) commonly-taught conventions, corroborated across commercial guides — with commercial/SEO-grade sources named only inside tier 3's expandable detail, each with its caveat printed, under a **no-affiliation / no-endorsement notice** (Wilhelmina, ABRSM, Aurora, John Casablancas, the FTC). The public page condenses the verification method to sourcing standards; the agent-voting mechanics stay internal.
- **A practitioner review gate (pre-ship):** a paid review/pilot of the drill set and gate criteria by at least one working runway coach or casting professional, findings folded into the F-record before launch [DESIGN — logged as a founder call below].
- **A measurement-candor gate (pre-"objective"):** per-channel repeatability/noise-floor figures published on the lineage page before the word "objective" appears in any copy; until then, "instrumented" or "measured" [DESIGN].
- An honest-limits statement on the box; measurable, outcome-only criteria [F12iii]; dated, scoped claims; the differentiator stated with its full scope: instrumented per-walk measurement with personal baselines and mastery records — **which none of the five competitors we surveyed in July 2026 documents** [F13c; the scope qualifiers are tags that must survive rewrite].
- **The unmeasured half of the craft has a structured home:** every level carries its labeled cue-only track (arms, hands, expression, posing repertoire — "coached, never scored" [P5]) plus a "what to take to a human coach" referral note. Teaching what we cannot measure, labeled, strengthens the honesty story; hiding it would invert pedagogy to fit hardware.

**Exact honesty disclosures — [F.40 DRAFT] (founder rewrite required; tags and qualifiers must survive the rewrite):**

Research on motor learning suggests that is a better indicator of what you've kept than your best in-session score. Both numbers are yours; only the cold one advances the record."
3. **Career** [F14a–c]: "This is practice equipment, not career preparation. We make no signing or career claims. 4. **Tempo** [P7, P2]: "Tempo targets are relative to your own baseline and delivered as music you feel, not numbers you chase. We publish no 'industry BPM' — none is credibly documented."
5. **Lineage** [F-record]: "Every drill on this syllabus names where it comes from — a named coach practice, a published study, or our own design choice, labeled as exactly that. Sources are tiered, and none of them is affiliated with or endorses this product."
6. **The graduation truth** [F14a, F14c — new]: "A record is practice, not a verdict. A runway walk is judged by human eyes — show it to people, not only to us. A mirror, a video, a coach see the whole body this headset cannot. Legitimate agencies review walks for free at open calls."
