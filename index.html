UNDERSTANDING THE SCORE

Here are how the 3 main metrics are defined, tracked, and what it means for you. Please note that the data for these metrics is tracked by the headset only.

PACE
head speed vs the target head speed
score = 100 within ±0.10 m/s of the target; slower costs 90 per m/s, faster costs 45 (40–100). In the band = 100.
In the industry's words: "take long strides, and control your pace" — Naomi Campbell
CARRIAGE
% of time the head stays level (downward glances negatively affect this score)
score = 40 + 60 × the share of the walk your head stayed level (chin within 7° of neutral) & facing down the line. A head-drop (>15° down) counts against it.
In the industry's words: "keep your head straight and steady" — CM Models
LINE
head alignment with the center line
score = 100 − (your average distance off the centerline, in meters) × 240  (40–100). Dead on the line = 100.
In the industry's words: "walking along a thin, invisible line" — Backstage
Computed on-device from Meta Quest 3 head tracking only — no camera, no body scan; nothing is scored that the headset did not measure.
